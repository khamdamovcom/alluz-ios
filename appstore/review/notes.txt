No account needed. All content is public and read-only; it comes from our website alluzbekistan.com.

1. Account and paid features: the app has no registration or login, no user-generated content, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads and no tracking. Every feature is available immediately after launch.

2. Purpose and audience: All Uzbekistan is a free, independent travel and reference guide to the whole of Uzbekistan, in English, Russian and Uzbek. It is for foreign travellers planning or making a trip and for local residents. It brings scattered, often single-language information together in one place: sights and monuments (including UNESCO sites), all 14 regions and their districts, hotels and restaurants with contacts and locations, history and culture, travel routes, live departure and arrival boards of Uzbekistan's airports, train timetables, visa rules, weather, the official Central Bank exchange rate, postal codes and government office contacts.

3. How to access the features: no login, credentials or setup are required. After a short welcome screen everything is available from the tab bar: Home, Explore (sights, regions, hotels, restaurants, culture, history), Map, Flights (live airport boards; trains, stations and city transport are under Explore > Transport) and More (search, saved places, weather, currency, visa, post, government offices, settings, about). The language can be changed in More > Settings. Location permission is optional; it is requested only when the user taps "My location" on the map and is used only on the device.

4. External services: our own server alluzbekistan.com provides all content to the app; Apple MapKit is used for the in-app map. Our server collects public data from official sources: Uzbekistan airports (flight boards), the Flightradar24 API (flight progress), Uzbekistan Railways (train timetable), MET Norway (weather forecast), the Central Bank of Uzbekistan (exchange rates), Uzbekistan Post (postal codes) and official government websites (visa rules, office contacts). Because cbu.uz is not reachable from servers outside Uzbekistan, the currency screen may also read the public exchange-rate JSON directly from cbu.uz on the device (read-only, no user data sent). Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr under Creative Commons licences and from official government websites, with author and licence shown under every photo. External links open Apple Maps, Google Maps or Yandex Maps for directions, the phone app for calls, and official websites (for example the railway ticket site) in Safari. The app does not use third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, tracking, sign-in services, payment processors or AI services.

5. Regional differences: the app works the same in every region where it is available; there are no region-specific features. The content is about Uzbekistan.

6. Regulated industries and third-party material: the app is not in a regulated industry and offers no financial transactions, health, gambling or similar services. The currency and bank sections are information only, based on the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's public data. Photos are used under Creative Commons or official licences with attribution; map data from Apple Maps. All other texts were written by the All Uzbekistan team.
