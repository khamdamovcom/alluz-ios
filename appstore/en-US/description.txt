All Uzbekistan is a free, independent guide to the whole country — from the Registan in Samarkand and the old town of Bukhara to Khiva's Itchan Kala, the Fergana Valley and the deserts of Karakalpakstan.

EXPLORE
• 400+ sights: madrasas, mausoleums, mosques, fortresses, museums, bazaars and nature — with photos, history and practical info (hours, tickets, how long to stay)
• All 14 regions and 200+ cities and districts
• UNESCO World Heritage sites
• Hotels, guesthouses, restaurants and teahouses with contacts
• Ready-made itineraries day by day

TRAVEL TOOLS
• Live departure and arrival boards for all 16 airports of Uzbekistan
• Train timetables and stations, bus stations, metro
• Visa rules for your citizenship and e-visa information
• Weather and climate for every region — when is the best time to go
• Central Bank exchange rates and a currency converter
• Distances between cities
• Post codes and government offices

CULTURE
• History from ancient times to today, with timelines

• Uzbek cuisine, crafts, traditions, holidays and festivals

DESIGNED FOR TRAVELLERS
